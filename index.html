About Me

Investigator's Notes

I'm a Cybersecurity undergraduate at MIVA Open University (CYB 305: Digital Forensics and Investigation Methods), building practical forensic capability across the full evidence lifecycle — acquisition, analysis, and courtroom presentation. My capstone work covers forensic disk imaging with verified integrity (FTK Imager), reconstruction of a multi-stage network intrusion from raw packet captures (Wireshark), and legal analysis of digital evidence admissibility under Nigeria's Evidence Act 2011, including the Supreme Court's landmark decision in Kubor v. Dickson. I believe good forensics is quiet, methodical, and brutally honest about its own limitations.

CASE FILE 002

Cyber Trail Reconstruction from Network Traffic & Logs

Reconstructed a simulated insider intrusion from a 69-packet Wireshark capture and corroborating attacker-side evidence across three attack stages.

  • Reconstructed timeline: nmap SYN scan → Hydra SSH brute force → wget HTTP data retrieval

CASE FILE 003

Legal Analysis & Admissibility of Digital Evidence

Analysed the Nigerian Supreme Court's landmark ruling in Kubor v. Dickson (2014) 4 NWLR (Pt. 1345) 534 SC on s.84 Evidence Act 2011.

  • Applied s.84(2) admissibility conditions condition-by-condition to the electronic exhibits
  • Evaluated forensic procedure failures: no chain of custody, no hashing, no foundation witness
  • Drafted courtroom-ready simulated expert witness statement with s.84(4) certificate and declaration of truth
  • Distinguished technical authenticity from legal admissibility — the case's central lesson
